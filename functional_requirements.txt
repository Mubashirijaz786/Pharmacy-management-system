PHARMACY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
Functional Requirements Document
2.1 Sales Management (POS Billing)
Generate fast POS invoices
Support barcode scanning for medicines
Allow manual product search
Add/remove items in cart
Apply:
Discounts (item-level & bill-level)
Taxes (GST/VAT if applicable)
Handle:
Cash payments
Card payments (optional integration)
Credit sales (customer ledger)
Generate printable invoice
Store all sales history
Support return & refund system
2.2 Medicine/Product Management
Add/Edit/Delete medicines
Medicine attributes:
Name
Generic name
Brand
Category (tablet, syrup, injection)
Strength (e.g., 500mg)
Price (cost & selling price)
Barcode generation & mapping
Medicine images (optional)
Drug type classification
2.3 Inventory / Stock Management
Real-time stock updates after every sale
Batch-wise stock tracking
Expiry date tracking per batch
Stock adjustment (damage, loss, manual correction)
Stock transfer between branches
Low stock alerts
Reorder level setup per product
2.4 Supplier Management
Store supplier details:
Name
Contact
Company
Address
Track supplier purchase history
Maintain supplier balance (credit system)
Generate supplier ledger reports
2.5 Customer Management
Customer registration
Purchase history tracking
Credit account system (if medicine is sold on loan)
Customer contact details
Loyalty/discount system (optional)
2.6 Prescription Handling
Prescription upload (image or PDF)
Link prescription with sale
Pharmacist verification module
Doctor details (optional)
Prescription history per customer
2.7 Pricing, Discount & Tax System
Flexible pricing rules:
Retail price
Wholesale price
Discount types:
Percentage discount
Fixed discount
Tax configuration per product or invoice
Automatic calculation of totals
2.8 Barcode Scanning Support
Support USB barcode scanner
Map barcode → product ID
Auto-add item to cart
Prevent duplicate scanning errors
2.9 Purchase Management (Procurement)
Create purchase orders
Receive stock from suppliers
Update inventory automatically on receiving goods
Track purchase invoices
Partial stock receiving support
Purchase return system
2.10 Reports Module
Sales Reports:
Daily / weekly / monthly sales
Total revenue
Profit & loss report
Inventory Reports:
Stock in hand
Expiry report
Low stock report
Finance Reports:
Customer credit report
Supplier payment report
Operational Reports:
Top selling medicines
Fast-moving vs slow-moving products
2.11 User Roles & Permissions
System must support role-based access:
Roles:
Admin
Cashier
Pharmacist
Manager
Permissions:
Admin: full access
Cashier: billing only
Pharmacist: prescription + medicine validation
Manager: reports + stock control
2.12 Multi-Branch Support (Optional Advanced)
Multiple store locations
Centralized database
Stock transfer between branches
Branch-wise reporting
2.13 Alerts & Notifications
Low stock warning
Expiry date alerts (30/60/90 days)
Out-of-stock alerts
Pending payments alerts
